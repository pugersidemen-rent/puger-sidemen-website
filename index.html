Rental Terms and Conditions

  1. An international driving license is required for foreigners, and a national driving license is required for Indonesians.
  2. The motorbike and the renter are not covered by any insurance. Any risk or accident is the responsibility of the renter.
  3. Motorbikes are rented for passengers only, not for cargo.
  4. All types of damage to the motorcycle parts (scratches, broken bodywork, missing spare parts, lost keys, torn tires) are the responsibility of the renter, who must replace them according to the same specifications.
  5. The renter does not receive a replacement motorbike in the event of an accident.
  6. Loss of the motorbike (due to theft, fire, accident, etc.) is the responsibility of the renter. The renter is obliged to replace the motorbike at the market price determined by the owner.
  7. Minimum rental: one day, for 24 hours (07:00 am to 07:00 am the next day).
  8. The renter is required to pay the vehicle rental fee at the start.
  9. Take photos and a video of the motorcycle before payment to document any existing scratches, damage, or missing spare parts.
  10. Gasoline is not included in the price.
